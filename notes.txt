Zorvix Labs Company Handbook

Founded: Zorvix Labs was founded in 2019 by Mira Okonkwo in Lisbon, Portugal.
The company builds offline-first note-taking software for field researchers.

Products:
Zorvix Notes is the flagship app. It stores data locally and syncs over Wi-Fi
only when the user taps "Sync Now". The free plan allows 3 notebooks. The Pro
plan costs 8 euros per month and allows unlimited notebooks.

Support policy:
Support hours are Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 Lisbon time. Weekend
tickets are answered on Monday. Refunds are available within 14 days of purchase.
Emergency contact for outages: outages@zorvix.example.

Remote work:
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. The annual learning budget
is 1,200 euros per person. The company retreat happens every September in Madeira.

Security:
All local data is encrypted with AES-256. The master key never leaves the device.
If a user forgets their password, the data cannot be recovered.
